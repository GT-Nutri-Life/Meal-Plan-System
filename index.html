How the systems connect

What each tool hands to the next, and the field names behind it.

Open any tool and work as usual. What you enter and what it calculates is lifted into a shared clinical record; the next tool offers to fill itself in from it. Nothing is sent anywhere until you are signed in, and then only to your own row in the practice database.
